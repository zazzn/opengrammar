Privacy Policy

OGrammar browser extension · Last updated June 2026

OGrammar is private by default. Its grammar, spelling, and style checking runs entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on the optional AI features with your own API key, and then only to the provider you choose.

What stays on your device

  • The core checker (grammar, spelling, style, and the writing score) runs locally using an on-device engine. The text in your editable fields is analyzed in your browser and never transmitted to OGrammar or any third party.
  • Your custom dictionary, ignored suggestions, per-site preferences, and settings are stored in your browser's own storage (synced through your Google account if you have Chrome Sync enabled, exactly like your bookmarks). We never receive them.
  • Usage analytics (counts of issues found, for your own writing-history view) are kept in your browser's local storage. They are not transmitted to us or anyone else.

What leaves your device (only if you enable AI)

The AI tiers (advanced corrections, tone rewriting, and optional autocomplete) are off by default and require you to add your own API key for a provider you choose (for example OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, Together, or a local Ollama server).

  • When you trigger an AI action, the relevant text (a sentence or selection, and for autocomplete a small amount of surrounding context) is sent directly from your device to the provider you configured, over your own account with that provider.
  • OGrammar has no servers. Your text and your key never pass through any OGrammar-operated service. The request goes straight to your chosen provider's API.
  • If you point OGrammar at a local Ollama server, even the AI tier stays on your own machine or network.

Your API keys

API keys you enter are encrypted at rest in your browser and are used only to authenticate your own requests to your chosen provider. They are never sent to OGrammar. You can remove all stored keys at any time from the extension's options page.

Permissions

The extension requests access to the pages you visit (<all_urls>) for a single purpose: to detect and correct writing issues in the editable text fields you type into, on whatever site you are using. It does not read, collect, or transmit page content for any other purpose, and it takes no action on a page until you type in a text field.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no user profiles.
  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics sent to us or any third party.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data. We never see your data, so there is nothing to sell.
  • No use of your text to train any model.

Third parties

The only third parties that can receive your text are the AI providers you explicitly configure, under your own account with them. Their handling of your data is governed by their privacy policies. Choose a provider you trust, or run a local model so nothing leaves your machine.

Open source

OGrammar is open source under the Apache License 2.0. You can inspect exactly what it does at github.com/zazzn/opengrammar.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Questions or concerns can be raised via the project's GitHub issues.

OGrammar is a privacy-first, local-first writing assistant. Your words are yours.
